### Feature cards (3-up grid)

1. **AI from your text**  
   Paste a chapter, article, or notes. Get flashcards you can edit before they land in a set.

2. **Reviews on a schedule**  
   Start with what’s due today. After each answer, the app plans the next review—no spreadsheet required.

3. **One place to learn**  
   Generate, organize sets, and study from your dashboard. Your library stays private to your account.
